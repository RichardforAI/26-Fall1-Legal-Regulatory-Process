1.1课程导论 · Course Introduction

▶1.1.1三大核心问题 Three core questions

本课程研究美国法律与规制程序，核心是回答三个问题，贯穿整个 MSL 项目，是理解美国「造法体系」（lawmaking system）的骨架。

This course examines the American legal and regulatory process. It revolves around three questions that run through the entire MSL program — the skeleton for understanding the American "lawmaking system."

1.1.1.1 法律如何制定？ How are laws made?

关注谁起草、谁通过、谁授权——立法机关、总统与行政机构如何共同「造法」。

This asks who drafts, who enacts, and who authorizes law — that is, how the legislature, the president, and the administrative agencies jointly "make" law through their respective roles.

1.1.1.2 法律如何实施？ How are they implemented?

关注负责执行法律的各政府机构如何把成文规则落到现实。

This asks how the various governmental institutions charged with applying law actually translate written rules into real-world practice and enforcement.

1.1.1.3 法律如何解释？ How are they interpreted?

当条文或规章含义模糊时，由谁、以何种原则确定其含义。

This asks who, and under what principles, determines the meaning of a statute or regulation when its language is ambiguous.

▶1.1.2造法流程（三方互动） Lawmaking flow

一部法律从无到有的完整旅程，展示立法、行政、司法三权如何共同参与造法。

The complete journey of a law from nothing to something, showing how all three branches — legislative, executive, and judicial — participate in lawmaking.

1.1.2.1 立法阶段 Legislative stage

立法者起草法案 → 国会通过制定法（statute）→ 送交总统签署。

Legislators draft a bill; Congress passes it into a statute; and if all goes well, it is sent to the president for signature.

1.1.2.2 行政阶段 Administrative stage

往往创设行政机构/监管局，负责将国会意志转化为实际执行的规章（regulations），并建立资金机制。

A new administrative agency or regulatory bureau is often created, charged with turning congressional will into concrete regulations and setting up a funding scheme to carry the venture forward.

1.1.2.3 司法阶段 Judicial stage

法院介入：解释条文中模糊语言，或审理个人为执行法定要求提起的诉讼。

Courts then enter the process — interpreting ambiguous statutory language, or hearing lawsuits brought by individuals seeking to enforce the statutory mandate.

1.1.2.4 例：自动驾驶汽车 Example: autonomous vehicles

平衡「便利 vs 公共安全」→ 国会立法 → 行政机构制定规章与资金 → 法院解释条款。造法从不是「国会通过一部法律」那么简单，而是三方持续互动。

To balance convenience against public safety: Congress legislates, agencies issue regulations and funding, and courts interpret the terms. Lawmaking is never simply "Congress passes a law" — it is a continuous, dynamic interaction among the three branches.

▶1.1.3核心概念：书本 vs 行动 Law on the books vs in action

1.1.3.1 书本上的法 Law on the books

写在纸面、印在法律书里的规则与造法制度。

The rules and structures that are written on paper and printed in the law books — the formal, written side of the legal system.

1.1.3.2 行动中的法 Law in action

法律本质上是社会建构，其真实面目体现在人类决策者（法官、官员）如何在约束条件下实际适用。教授强调「保持真实」（keeping it real）——理解现实世界才能理解法律。

Law is fundamentally also a social construct, best understood by how human decision-makers (judges, officials) actually apply it under conditions of constraint. The instructor stresses "keeping it real" — to understand how law works, you must understand the real world.

▶1.1.4典型案例：大麻 Marijuana / federalism conflict

1.1.4.1 联邦立场 Federal position

数十年来大麻是附表 I（Schedule I）管制物质，无论数量，持有、销售、分销均非法。

For decades the federal law has been clear: marijuana is a Schedule I controlled substance, illegal to possess (in any amount), sell, or distribute.

1.1.4.2 州的现实 State reality

越来越多州将大麻合法化（先医疗、后娱乐用途）。

Yet a growing number of states have legalized marijuana — first for medical use, and more recently for recreational use.

1.1.4.3 冲突与原因 Conflict & reasons

冲突本质：联邦法（书本上的法）vs 州法/现实（行动中的法）。联邦容忍的可能原因：① 执法裁量权；② 联邦制下更适合在州/地方处理。

The essence is the clash between "law on the books" (federal prohibition) and "law in action" (state legalization). Why does the federal government tolerate it? (1) law-enforcement discretion — prosecuting small-time users is neither economical nor socially acceptable; and (2) a broader judgment that, under federalism, such questions are best handled at the state and local level.

1.2法律渊源与普通法 · Sources of Law & Common Law

▶1.2.1法律渊源等级（由高到低） Hierarchy of sources

法官不能凭空造法，必须「寻找并适用控制性法律」（controlling law）。美国法律渊源由高到低：

Judges cannot simply make up law; they must "ferret out and apply" the controlling law. American sources of law, from highest to lowest, are:

1.2.1.1 宪法（至上条款） Constitution (Supremacy Clause)

第六条宣布宪法及依宪法程序制定的法律、条约为「全国最高法律」（supreme law of the land）。

Article VI declares the Constitution, and the laws and treaties made under it, to be the "supreme law of the land" — the highest source of law.

1.2.1.2 联邦法律 / 条约 Federal statutes / treaties

但联邦法受宪法列举事项限制——如「野生动物财产权」不在其中，国会无权分配纽约州海滩狐狸的归属。

But federal law's reach is limited by the Constitution to enumerated subjects. Property in wild animals is not among them, so Congress had no business allocating rights in foxes on a New York beach.

1.2.1.3 州法与市政法律 State & municipal law

联邦制允许州在多数财产权领域立法；宪法与制定法都无规定时（法律空白 lacuna），诉诸普通法（判例法）。

Federalism lets states legislate in most property areas. When constitutions and statutes are silent — a "lacuna" — courts turn to the common law (decisional or case law).

▶1.2.2普通法（规则+方法+历史） Common law: rules & method

1.2.2.1 双重含义 Two meanings

普通法既是「一套法律规则」，也是「一套分析方法」，发端于中世纪英格兰王室法院，是法官与律师间的「对话」产物。

The common law is both a set of legal rules and a method of analysis. It was forged in the English royal courts in the Middle Ages, beginning as a dialogue between judges and lawyers.

1.2.2.2 构成要素 Components

理性（reason）、自然法、逻辑、习惯（customs）、先例（precedent）及对制定法的解释。

Reason, natural law, logic, custom, precedent, and the interpretation of statutes were all used by litigants — and so can all be regarded as components of the common law.

1.2.2.3 宪政维度与历史 Constitutional dimension & history

普通法还含「君主权力非绝对」原则——税收需议会同意、1215《大宪章》（Magna Carta）禁止不经审判监禁，影响了美国革命与宪法。独立后各州「继受」并「美国化」英格兰普通法。

It also embodied the principle that monarchical power is not absolute — taxation required Parliament's consent, and the Magna Carta (1215) barred imprisonment without trial. These ideas shaped the American Revolution and Constitution. After independence, the states "received" and then "Americanized" the English common law.

▶1.2.3普通法推理工具 Reasoning tools

1.2.3.1 先例 / 遵循先例 Precedent / stare decisis

判决正确的案件加入普通法体系，成为先例，用以解决类似纠纷（未必有拘束力，但会被援引）。

Correctly-decided cases are added to the body of common law as precedent; though not necessarily binding, they are used to resolve similar disputes.

1.2.3.2 区分案件 Distinguishing

当先例与本案事实相似但不完全相同，律师可主张「区分」以规避先例的拘束。

Where a precedent is factually similar but not identical, a lawyer may "distinguish" the case at bar — pointing out differences of fact or circumstance to avoid being bound by it.

1.2.3.3 判决理由 Ratio decidendi

拘束力来自判例中的「逻辑/理由」，而非结果——推理错误的案件不构成先例（Sir Edward Coke 语）。

What binds is the logic or reasoning within the past case (the ratio decidendi), not the bare result. A wrongly-reasoned case is not precedent — as Sir Edward Coke observed, precedent binds because of the reasoned opinion, not merely because it was decided.

1.2.3.4 首例案件 Case of first impression

无先例可循，法院须另寻法理（博学评注、政策考量）；亦可推翻先例或前瞻性推翻。

With no precedent to follow, the court must seek law elsewhere (learned commentary, policy concerns). A court may also overrule precedent, or apply the old law now while announcing a different rule prospectively.

▶1.2.4Pierson v. Post（1805 狐狸案） The fox case
案情 Facts

猎人 Post 追猎狐狸，即将得手时被闯入者 Pierson 抢走。初审判归 Post；纽约州最高法院改判归 Pierson。

Hunter Post chased a fox; just as he was about to catch it, interloper Pierson grabbed it and carried it away. The trial court awarded the fox to Post, but the NY Supreme Court reversed — awarding it to Pierson.

1.2.4.1 法律争点 Issue

人何时对野生动物取得财产权？（也是知识产权法的本质问题：创意何时受保护）

When does a person acquire property in a wild animal? (This is also the essence of intellectual-property law: when does a "floating" idea become protectable?)

1.2.4.2 最终规则 Rule

采纳 Barbeyrac 观点：野生动物在被「击伤、围困或捕获……以致丧失天然自由」时才取得所有权。

Adopting Barbeyrac's view, the majority held that ownership in a wild animal occurs when it is "wounded, circumvented or ensnared … so as to deprive them of their natural liberty."

1.2.4.3 政策之争 Policy debate

  • 多数（Tompkins）：明确界限规则（bright-line），行政效率高、易执行。
  • 异议（Livingston）：经济效率——奖励猎人劳动、鼓励投资创业；预示现代 ADR。

Majority (Tompkins): a bright-line rule that is easy to apply and administratively efficient. Dissent (Livingston): economic efficiency — rewarding the hunter's labor and encouraging enterprise; his comment also foreshadows modern ADR.

▶1.2.5Popov v. Hayashi（2002 棒球案） The baseball case
案情 Facts

Barry Bonds 破纪录本垒打球飞入 Popov 手套，遭球迷袭击脱手，滚入 Hayashi 手中。Popov 诉返还。

Barry Bonds' record home-run ball landed in Popov's glove, but a mob knocked it loose and it rolled into Hayashi's hands. Popov sued for conversion.

1.2.5.1 区分案件 Distinguishing

法院采纳 Gray 规则（占有须「保持控制」），并区分 Pierson（放宽标准的情境不适用于棒球）。

The court adopted Gray's Rule — possession requires retaining control — and distinguished Pierson, whose relaxed standard (for a harpooned whale, fleeing fox, or sunken ship) does not apply to a baseball.

1.2.5.2 占有前利益 + 衡平分割 Pre-possessory interest & equitable division

Popov 被暴力剥夺控制，法院创设「占有前利益」（不能让结果由暴力决定）；双方均无过错，球须出售、价款平分。

Because Popov was deprived of control by violence, the court created a "pre-possessory interest" (the result must not be dictated by violence). Since both parties were innocent victims, it ordered equitable division — the ball sold and the proceeds split equally.

1.3宪政体系与解释 · Constitutional System & Interpretation

▶1.3.1三大结构原则 Three structural principles

1.3.1.1 联邦制 Federalism

纵向分权（国家 vs 州），通过「分割」限制政府权力。

The vertical division of power between the national and state governments, limiting governmental power by fragmenting it.

1.3.1.2 三权分立 Separation of powers

横向制衡（checks and balances），各平等分支需相互同意才能行动。

The horizontal division of power among the three co-equal federal branches, creating checks and balances that require the concurrence of the others.

1.3.1.3 至上条款 Supremacy Clause

联邦宪法、法律、条约优先于相冲突的州法。

Federal law — the Constitution, statutes, and treaties — prevails over conflicting state law.

宪法是「授权文件」（document of grant）：国家政府只有宪法授予的权力；第十修正案把未授予的权力保留给州或人民。

The Constitution is a "document of grant" — the national government has only the powers delegated to it; the Tenth Amendment reserves all other powers to the states or the people.

▶1.3.2宪法条文三类 Three types of provisions

1.3.2.1 具体规则 Specific rules

总统须出生公民、年满 35 岁、最多两届（无歧义，可能有争议）。

The president must be a natural-born citizen, at least 35 years old, and serve no more than two terms — rules with no ambiguity (though possibly controversy).

1.3.2.2 相当具体 Reasonably specific

言论自由（内容中立、观点中立、时间地点方式限制）。

Free speech, with its built-in principles of content and viewpoint neutrality and "time, place, and manner" restrictions — provisions that still require some judicial judgment.

1.3.2.3 开放式 Open-ended

第八修正案「残酷异常刑罚」、正当程序（what process is due）、平等保护（最棘手）。

The Eighth Amendment's "cruel and unusual punishment," due process ("what process is due"), and equal protection — the most vexing open-ended provision, whose text offers little guidance.

谁解释？三个分支都宣誓捍卫宪法；但法院通常有「最后发言权」（last word）。

Who interprets? All three branches take an oath to the Constitution, but the courts generally have the last word.

▶1.3.3关键权力 Key powers

1.3.3.1 商业条款 Commerce Clause

  • Wickard v. Filburn (1942)：聚合效应（自用小麦也影响州际市场）——最广读法。
  • Lopez (1995) / Morrison (2000)：收窄——须经济/商业性质。
  • NFIB v. Sebelius (2012)：不能规制「不行为」（inactivity）。
  • Gonzales v. Raich (2005)：纯州内大麻也可被联邦规制。

Wickard (1942) established the "aggregate effects" test — even home-consumed wheat affects the interstate market. Lopez (1995) and Morrison (2000) narrowed the power to economic/commercial activity. NFIB v. Sebelius (2012) barred regulating "inactivity." Raich (2005) held that even purely intrastate marijuana is regulable.

1.3.3.2 必要与适当条款 Necessary and Proper Clause

McCulloch v. Maryland (1819)：只要「目的合法＋手段适当」，即为合宪。

McCulloch v. Maryland (1819): so long as the end is legitimate and the means are appropriate and plainly adapted to it, the law is constitutional.

1.3.3.3 司法审查 Judicial review

「阐明法律是什么，是司法部门的职责」——「与宪法抵触的法律无效」。也带来 Bickel 的「反多数难题」（countermajoritarian difficulty）。

"It is emphatically the province and duty of the judicial department to say what the law is" — and "a law repugnant to the Constitution is void." This also raises Bickel's "countermajoritarian difficulty": unelected judges have the final word.

Marbury v. Madison（1803，司法审查之母）

1800 年大选失利前夕，亚当斯政府任命一批「午夜法官」（midnight judges），但马伯里的委任状未及送达。新任国务卿麦迪逊拒绝送达，马伯里依《1789 司法法》直接诉至最高法院，要求发「执行令」（writ of mandamus）。

On the eve of losing the 1800 election, the Adams administration appointed "midnight judges," but Marbury's commission was never delivered. New Secretary of State Madison refused to deliver it; Marbury sued in the Supreme Court under the Judiciary Act of 1789, seeking a writ of mandamus.

马歇尔首席大法官判决：① 马伯里有权得到委任；② 法律应给救济；③ 但《司法法》授予最高法院的「初审权」违宪——宪法是最高法，与之抵触的法律无效。由此确立司法审查：法院有权宣布违宪法律无效。

Chief Justice Marshall held: (1) Marbury had a right to the commission; (2) the law must afford a remedy; (3) but the Judiciary Act's grant of original jurisdiction was unconstitutional — the Constitution is supreme, and a law repugnant to it is void. This established judicial review.

▶1.3.4权利法案（前十修正案） Bill of Rights
  • 第一：宗教（政教分离/自由行使）、言论、集会、请愿 religion, speech, assembly, petition
  • 第二：持有与携带武器 keep and bear arms
  • 第四五六：刑事司法保护 criminal-justice protections
  • 第五：正当程序（未经正当程序不得剥夺生命、自由、财产） due process of law
  • 第七：陪审团 jury trial
  • 第八：禁止残酷异常刑罚 no cruel and unusual punishment

应「整体地」（holistically）看待权利法案乃至整部宪法——它们共同构成公民权利与自由的基本架构。

The Bill of Rights — and indeed the whole Constitution — should be viewed holistically, as providing the basic architecture of American civil rights and liberties.

▶1.3.5第十四修正案 Fourteenth Amendment

1.3.5.1 正当程序 Due process

程序性＋实体性；合并原则（incorporation）把权利法案适用于州；经济（Lochner 1905，后被摒弃）→ 个人（Lawrence 2003）。

Due process is both procedural and substantive. The "incorporation" doctrine applies virtually all Bill of Rights protections against the states. Its substantive focus shifted from economic liberty (Lochner 1905, later repudiated) to personal rights (Lawrence 2003).

1.3.5.2 平等保护 Equal protection

Civil Rights Cases (1883) → Plessy「隔离但平等」(1896) → Brown「本质上不平等」(1954)；平权行动（Gratz/Fisher）；性别歧视中间审查（VMI 1996）。

From the Civil Rights Cases (1883) to Plessy's "separate but equal" (1896) to Brown's "inherently unequal" (1954); affirmative action (Gratz/Fisher); and intermediate scrutiny for gender discrimination (VMI 1996).

▶1.3.6隐私权 Privacy
  • 第九修正案：暗示存在未列举的权利。 The Ninth Amendment implies unenumerated rights exist.
  • Griswold (1965)：从宪法「半影」（penumbra）确立隐私权。 Griswold located a right to privacy in the Constitution's "penumbra."
  • Roe v. Wade (1973)：堕胎权；Obergefell (2015)：同性婚姻；Bostock (2020)：LGBTQ 职场保护。 Roe (abortion), Obergefell (same-sex marriage), Bostock (LGBTQ workplace protection).
▶1.3.7解释哲学之争 Interpretive debate

宪法解释两派：原旨主义（originalism）只保护制宪者原本明确阐述的权利，变化应诉诸修宪（旗手 Scalia）；活的宪法（living constitution）视权利法案为「有机的」，可适用于现代情境。

Two constitutional camps: originalism protects only rights the Framers expressly elaborated (championed by Justice Scalia); the "living constitution" view treats the Bill of Rights as organic, adaptable to modern contexts.

制定法解释（statutory interpretation）的流派还包括：

Statutory-interpretation schools also include:

  • 文本主义（textualism）：只看法律条文的普通含义，不探究立法意图（Scalia 亦倡导）。 Textualism — look only to the ordinary meaning of the statutory text, not legislative intent.
  • 目的解释（purposivism）：探究立法背后所欲实现的「目的/宗旨」来解释条文。 Purposivism — interpret the text by reference to the law's broader purpose or spirit.

1.4联邦政府 · Federal Government

▶1.4.1三权分立 Separation of powers

1.4.1.1 立法 Legislative

国会 = 参议院 100 + 众议院 435，制定法律、批准法官、预算、宣战。

Congress (Senate 100 + House 435) makes the laws, approves judges, passes the budget, and declares war.

1.4.1.2 行政 Executive

总统 + 副总统 + 内阁（Cabinet），执行法律，雇员超 400 万。

The President, Vice President, and Cabinet enforce the law; the branch employs over four million people.

1.4.1.3 司法 Judicial

法院至最高法院，解释法律，9 名大法官终身任职（唯一终身制职位）。

The courts, up to the Supreme Court, interpret the law; its nine justices serve for life — the only life-tenured office.

制衡理念：「野心用野心对抗」（ambition counteracts ambition，麦迪逊语）。

The underlying idea is checks and balances — "ambition counteracts ambition" (Madison).

▶1.4.2选举程序 Electoral process

1.4.2.1 众议院 The House

435 名众议员，名额多年固定，如今每位众议员代表约 77 万公民。每区选一人（单一选区制 single-member district），任期 2 年、可无限连任（John Dingell 服务 60 年）。

435 representatives — a number fixed for years — so each now represents ~770,000 citizens. Each district elects one member (single-member district); terms are 2 years with no term limit (John Dingell served 60 years).

麦迪逊曾称，众议院的价值在于议员拥有「对本地选民的恰当了解」（proper knowledge of the local circumstances of their constituents）——但如今选区人口巨大，这一点已难成立。

Madison praised the House for members' "proper knowledge of the local circumstances of their local constituents" — implausible today given how large districts have become.

1.4.2.2 参议院 The Senate

每州 2 名、共 100 人，无论人口（怀俄明与加州同为 2 席，尽管加州人口近 60 倍）。任期 6 年、无任期限制（Robert Byrd 服务 51 年）。第 17 修正案（1913）之前，参议员由州议会选出，之后改由人民直选。

Two per state, 100 total, regardless of population (Wyoming equals California, though California is nearly 60× larger). 6-year terms, no term limit (Robert Byrd served 51 years). Before the 17th Amendment (1913), state legislatures — not the people — elected senators.

1.4.2.3 一人一票原则 One person, one vote

1960 年代最高法院（Baker v. Carr / Reynolds v. Sims）依「平等保护条款」确立：立法者代表的是人，不是「树木或土地」（people, not trees or acres），选区人口须实质均等——因此每 10 年须人口普查。参议院每州 2 席是这一原则的刻意例外（制宪者要保各州平等影响力）。

In the 1960s the Supreme Court (Baker v. Carr / Reynolds v. Sims) held, under the Equal Protection Clause, that "legislators represent people, not trees or acres" — districts must be roughly equal in population, hence the decennial census. The Senate's two-per-state rule is a deliberate exception, preserving equal state influence.

1.4.2.4 现任者优势 Incumbency

连任率：参议院 87%、众议院 95%+。四大原因：① 知名度（name recognition）；② 资金（胜选参议员平均花 $1000 万、众议员 $150 万，2016 宾州一席达 $2800 万）；③ 服务选民（「美国人恨国会，却爱自己的议员」）；④ 选区划分（gerrymandering 锁定优势）。

Re-election rates: 87% (Senate), 95%+ (House). Four reasons: (1) name recognition; (2) money — winning Senate candidates spend ~$10M and House ~$1.5M (one 2016 PA seat cost $28M); (3) constituent service — "Americans hate Congress but love their member"; (4) gerrymandering locks in advantage.

1.4.2.5 总统选举人团 Electoral College

第 22 修正案（1951）限总统两届（因 FDR 曾连任四次）。选举人团 538 人（众 435 + 参 100 + 特区 3），获 270 票当选。除两州外采「赢者通吃」；选举人理论上可反水（失信选举人，极少发生）。2016（特朗普）与 2000（小布什）都出现普选票多却输选举人团。若无候选人过半数，由众议院选总统。改革为直接普选的呼声至今未果。

The 22nd Amendment (1951) caps presidents at two terms (after FDR's four wins). The Electoral College has 538 electors (House 435 + Senate 100 + DC 3); 270 votes win. All but two states use "winner-take-all"; electors may in theory go "faithless" (rare). In 2016 (Trump) and 2000 (Bush) the popular-vote winner lost. If no one reaches a majority, the House picks the president. Calls to switch to a direct popular vote remain unheeded.

▶1.4.3党派 Partisanship

两党制（民主党/共和党）高度极化，两党渐被推向意识形态两端，跨党妥协愈发困难——政治学者称此困境为「僵局」（gridlock）。

A robust two-party system has polarized to ideological extremes, making cross-aisle compromise hard — what political scientists call "gridlock."

  • 典型案例：2016 奥巴马提名 Merrick Garland 填补 Scalia 空缺，因党派僵局失败，该席位最终由特朗普提名的 Neil Gorsuch 以微弱票数填补。 Obama's 2016 Garland nomination (after Scalia's death) failed in gridlock; Trump later filled it with Gorsuch by a close vote.
  • 多数党权力：多数党控制国会领导权、委员会任命与议程；总统党籍决定立法议程方向。 The majority party controls leadership, committee assignments, and the agenda; the president's party shapes lawmaking objectives.
  • 轮替：2016 共和党掌控两院+总统+多数州；2008–2010 则是民主党掌控两院+白宫——单党主导通常不会持久。 2016: Republicans held both houses, the presidency, and most states; 2008–2010: Democrats held both houses and the White House — one-party dominance rarely lasts.

1.5州与地方政府 · State & Local Government

▶1.5.1警察权 Police power

州拥有规制健康、安全、福利（health, safety, welfare）的广泛造法权——「警察权」（police power），是第十修正案「剩余权力归州/人民」的体现。大部分日常法律来自州与地方，主要包括：

States hold the "police power" — broad authority to regulate health, safety, and welfare — reflecting the Tenth Amendment's reservation of powers to the states. Most everyday law comes from state and local governments, including:

  • 执法（law enforcement） police and criminal law
  • K-12 教育（public schooling）
  • 土地使用与分区（land use / zoning）
  • 安全规章（safety rules）
  • 执照——驾照、营业执照等（licensing）

你几乎每天都与州/地方权威打交道：办驾照、向地方机构申请加盖房屋、办小企业执照、上社区学院——这些都是「行动中的法」（law in action）。

You deal with state and local authority almost daily: getting a driver's license, seeking permission to build an addition, licensing a small business, attending a community college — all "law in action."

▶1.5.2州政府结构 State structure

1.5.2.1 立法 Legislature

两院制（除内布拉斯加一院制）、两院都须「一人一票」（与联邦参议院的例外不同）。

Bicameral (except Nebraska's unicameral legislature); unlike the U.S. Senate, both state houses must meet the "one person, one vote" equality requirement.

1.5.2.2 行政（多元行政） Plural executive

州检察长、副州长等由人民分别直选，可与州长不同党，州长政策权力因此受限。

Officials like the attorney general and lieutenant governor are elected separately by the people — possibly of a different party — so the governor's policy power is more limited than the president's.

1.5.2.3 司法 Judiciary

州最高法院管理州司法，州法官常由人民直选（甚至党派选举）。

A state supreme court manages judicial business; unlike federal judges, state judges are often directly elected — sometimes in partisan elections.

▶1.5.3地方政府 Local government

1.5.3.1 法律地位 Legal status

是「州政府的产物」（creatures of state government），可由州创设/解散。

Local governments are "creatures of state government" — created and dissolved by state legislative decisions.

1.5.3.2 治理模式 Governance models

强市长制（strong-mayor）vs 城市经理制（city-manager）；地方自治（home rule）决定市权大小。

Two models: the strong-mayor system and the city-manager system; "home rule" determines how much authority a municipality has.

1.5.3.3 专门机构与财政 Specialized bodies & finance

学区委员会、分区委员会、跨界区域性机构；财政高度依赖财产税（property tax）、销售税、债券。

School boards, zoning boards, and regional authorities handle specialized functions; local finance relies heavily on property taxes, sales taxes, and bonds.

1.6联邦制 · Federalism

▶1.6.1定义与理由 Definition & rationale

1.6.1.1 定义 Definition

联邦制指州与联邦的纵向法律关系（vs 三权分立的横向）。

Federalism is the vertical legal relationship between state and national governments (contrasted with the horizontal separation of powers).

1.6.1.2 两大理由 Two rationales

① 限制政府权力（防「无代表征税」式强大中央干预）；② 决策贴近人民（地方知识）。

① It limits government power — guarding against broad central intervention of the sort that provoked "taxation without representation." ② It keeps decision-making close to the people, where local knowledge resides.

▶1.6.2结构保障 Structural protection
  • 参议院：每州 2 名，任何立法须两院一致同意。 The Senate — two per state — gives states a structural safeguard, since legislation requires both houses.
  • 选举人团：给各州在总统选举中的重要角色。 The Electoral College gives states an important role in choosing the president.
▶1.6.3商业条款冲突 Commerce Clause conflicts

商业条款是联邦—州冲突的主战场：新政（1930s–40s）后联邦连连获胜；1990 年代 Lopez（校园持枪）叫停。冲突由法院解决——联邦制「充满活力却也并不稳定」。

The Commerce Clause is the main battleground: after the New Deal (1930s–40s) the federal government won consistently, until Lopez (guns near schools) halted the streak in the 1990s. Courts resolve these conflicts — federalism is "alive but unstable."

▶1.6.4Gonzales v. Raich（2005）案例 Gonzales v. Raich
案情 Facts

加州《慈悲使用法》(1996) 允许医用大麻。Raich 与 Monson 自种自用；DEA 查抄销毁 Monson 的 6 株大麻。二人诉联邦《管制物质法》(CSA) 违反商业条款。第九巡回法院判其胜诉；最高法院撤销。

California's Compassionate Use Act (1996) authorized medical marijuana. Raich and Monson grew and used it themselves; the DEA seized and destroyed Monson's six plants. They sued, arguing the Controlled Substances Act (CSA) exceeded the Commerce Clause. The Ninth Circuit sided with them; the Supreme Court reversed.

1.6.4.1 判决（6–3，Stevens） Holding

CSA 合宪。三步：① 大麻存在巨大（非法）州际市场；② 自种自用聚合上影响市场供需（Wickard）；③ 只需「理性基础」（rational basis）。并区分 Lopez/Morrison（整体违宪 vs 具体适用）。

The CSA is constitutional. Three steps: (1) there is a huge (illegal) interstate marijuana market; (2) homegrown use, in the aggregate, affects supply and demand (Wickard); (3) only a "rational basis" is required. It also distinguished Lopez/Morrison as facial challenges, whereas this was an as-applied challenge.

1.6.4.2 各意见 Opinions

  • 协同（Scalia）：依据「必要与适当条款」而非商业条款本身。 Scalia concurred, resting the power on the Necessary and Proper Clause rather than the Commerce Clause alone.
  • 异议（Thomas）：「既非州际也非商业」。 Thomas dissented: the conduct is "neither interstate nor commerce."
  • 异议（O'Connor）：无证据即等于「一般警察权」，侵蚀州主权与「实验室」价值。 O'Connor dissented: without evidence, this grants a general "police power" that erodes state sovereignty and the states' role as "laboratories."
▶1.6.5思考题解答 Answers to guiding questions

Q1 核心分歧？ 商业条款的外部边界——「聚合效应＋理性基础」能否触及纯州内、非商业、自用、州法授权的活动？

Q2 哪方更有说服力？ 开放题：多数更贴合判例且务实；异议更忠于联邦制结构。

Q3 联邦权威是否减弱？ 事实层执法收缩、法律层权力未变（至上条款）。

Q4 是否应随时代变？ 活的宪法 vs 原旨主义之争。

Q1: the outer boundary of the Commerce Clause — can "aggregate effects + rational basis" reach purely intrastate, noncommercial, state-authorized activity? Q2: open — the majority is more faithful to precedent and more pragmatic, while the dissent is more faithful to structural federalism. Q3: enforcement has contracted in fact, but the constitutional power is unchanged (Supremacy Clause). Q4: the living-constitution vs originalism debate.

2.1三权分立 · Separation of Powers

▶2.1.1概念：双重制衡的两道刹车 The two checks on power

上一节讲联邦制如何通过中央—地方分权限制政府权力；2.1 讲的是横向的另一重制衡——三权分立。二者是同一枚硬币的两面，目标一致：限制国家权力、保护个人自由。

Federalism and the separation of powers are two sides of the same coin — both limit national power and protect individual liberty.

2.1.1.1 联邦制（纵向） Federalism (vertical)

联邦 vs 州：通过「分割」把权力纵向摊开，防止强大中央干预。

The vertical legal relationship between national and state governments — limiting power by fragmenting it.

2.1.1.2 三权分立（横向） Separation of powers (horizontal)

立法 vs 行政 vs 司法：通过「制衡」把权力横向摊开，各分支须相互同意才能行动。

The horizontal division among the three co-equal federal branches, creating checks and balances that require their mutual concurrence.

▶2.1.2制宪者的核心担忧 The framers' concern

制宪者（framers）最担心的是权力集中于单一政府部门，该部门就会「横行霸道」（run roughshod），践踏个人自由。

The framers were most concerned about the risk of power being consolidated in a single branch, which could run roughshod over individual liberty.

2.1.2.1 权力集中的风险 The risk of consolidation

单一部门掌权，即可肆意侵犯个人自由与权利。

A single branch holding power could trample individual liberty and freedom at will.

2.1.2.2 历史背景：英国君主制 Context: the British monarchy

这些早期美国人是在反抗他们所认为的专制中央政权——英国君主制。

These early Americans were rebelling against what they saw as an authoritarian central government — the British monarchy.

2.1.2.3 对策：分权 Solution: divide power

抵御此风险的最佳办法，制宪者认为，就是把权力分散、分开到不同部门。

The best way to guard against this risk, the framers thought, was to distribute and separate power among the branches.

▶2.1.3麦迪逊《联邦党人文集》第 51 篇 Federalist No. 51

本节最核心的引文，也是美国宪法学的经典金句，出自 James Madison：

The most important passage of this lesson, from James Madison, is the classic statement of the American constitutional system:

Ambition must be made to counteract ambition.必须以野心对抗野心。

2.1.3.1 部门独立 Departmental independence

各部门成员应「尽可能少地依赖其他部门」——否则其独立性「只是名义上的」。

Members of each department should be as little dependent as possible on the others; otherwise their independence would be merely nominal.

2.1.3.2 最大的保障 The great security

防权力逐渐集中，靠赋予各部门管理者「必要的宪法手段与个人动机去抵抗其他部门的侵犯」。防御须与受攻击的危险相称。

The great security against gradual concentration of power lies in giving each department the constitutional means and personal motives to resist encroachments; the defense must be commensurate to the danger of attack.

2.1.3.3 对人性的反思 A reflection on human nature

「政府本身若不是对人性最大的反思，又是什么？」——制度不指望官员是圣人，而用「争权」去互相抵消。

"What is government itself but the greatest of all reflections on human nature?" The system does not expect officials to be angels; it turns ambition into a self-policing mechanism.

▶2.1.4宪法文本结构 Constitutional structure (Articles I–III)

三权分立直接写在宪法前三条，各部门「不应越界」（should not get out of their lane）：

Separation of powers is written into the first three articles; each branch should stay in its lane:

条款 Article权力 Power归属 Branch
第一条 Article I立法权 legislative power国会 Congress
第二条 Article II行政权 executive power总统 President
第三条 Article III司法权 judicial power法院 Courts
▶2.1.5核心难题：边界并不清晰 The blurry boundary

但法律从来没那么简单。宪法并未精确定义何为「立法权」、何为「行政权」，因此需要解释（interpretation）。

But law is never quite so simple. The Constitution does not define with any precision what is legislative or executive power — this requires interpretation.

法院为「如何界定立法权与行政权」这一问题纠结了两百多年。光靠宪法文本中的三权分立结构，远远不足以解决这些模糊问题。

Courts have struggled for more than two centuries with how best to define legislative and executive power. The textual structure alone is not nearly enough to settle these questions.

▶2.1.6经典例子：行政机构算什么权力？ Administrative agency example

问题：设立一个行政机构（如专利商标局），让它制定规范专利商标系统的规章，属于立法权还是行政权？

Question: is creating an administrative agency (e.g. for patents and trademarks) that issues binding rules an exercise of legislative or executive power?

2.1.6.1 认为是立法权 The legislative view

若它在创设约束个人行为的规则，就像立法机关，属立法权范畴 → 国会保留完全控制权。

If it creates actual rules governing individual conduct, it acts like a legislature — so Congress retains full control.

2.1.6.2 认为是行政权 The executive view

若总统被授权任命该机构成员、并可决定罢免不称职官员，就明确属于第二条的行政权。

If the president appoints its members and can remove officials he dislikes, it is squarely within Article II's executive power.

结论：视频不解决这个问题，但想说明——答案一点也不显而易见。

The lesson does not resolve it, but the answer is not at all obvious.

▶2.1.7谁来「执法」三权分立？ Judicial resolution of conflicts

三权分立得以维护的一个重要方式，是通过司法裁决国会与总统之间出现的冲突。有些冲突在美国历史中打得相当激烈。

An important way separation of powers is policed is through judicial resolution of conflicts between Congress and the president — some quite hard-fought throughout American history.

▶2.1.8两个历史案例 Two landmark cases
① United States v. Nixon（1974 水门录音带案）

尼克松总统试图抵制国会限制其裁量权的努力（最终失败）。最高法院要求尼克松交出涉及水门事件的录音带，并以宪法三权分立的名义这么做——证明总统的「行政特权」并非绝对，司法可制衡总统。

President Nixon resisted Congress's attempts to limit his discretion (ultimately unsuccessfully). The Supreme Court required Nixon to turn over the Watergate tapes, in the name of the Constitution's separation of powers — showing that executive privilege is not absolute and that the judiciary can check the president.

② Morrison v. Olson（1988 独立检察官案）

最高法院支持了设立「独立检察官」制度的法律，用于调查政府官员渎职，尽管有人反对该制度会干扰三权分立——显示三权分立在实践中允许一定的「交叉」，并非绝对刚性。

The Court upheld a law establishing an independent counsel to investigate governmental misconduct, despite the objection that the scheme would interfere with the separation of powers — showing that separation of powers tolerates some overlap and is not rigidly absolute.

▶2.1.9结论 Conclusion
  • 对国会、总统、司法三大国家权力分支的行为提供有力的制衡（robust check）。 It provides a robust check on the behavior of the three named branches.
  • 提供一种机制，制约联邦体系中的监管机构（regulatory agencies）（后续课程详谈）。 It provides a mechanism to check regulatory agencies in the federal system.
  • 制宪者深知，正是通过三权分立，才能有效限制国家政府不必要地干涉个人自由。 The framers understood that separation of powers effectively limits unnecessary interference with individual liberty.
▶2.1.10讨论题解答 Discussion questions

讨论提示实为两个问题，非一个：

The discussion prompt is actually two questions, not one:

2.1.10.1 Q1：你的初步想法是什么？ What are your initial thoughts?

This is open and subjective — first reactions matter more than a "correct" answer. Possible angles:

  • 从「人」出发：麦迪逊「If men were angels…」→ 制度承认人的不可靠，用「争权」互相抵消。 Angle A (human nature): the system assumes people are not angels and uses ambition to counteract ambition.
  • 从「经验对比」出发：三权分立与联邦制都源于同一种恐惧——权力集中（英国君主制）。 Angle B (comparison): both doctrines guard against the same fear of concentrated power.
  • 从「模糊性」出发：连「立法权/行政权」都分不清，三权分立到底还能约束什么？ Angle C (ambiguity): if the branches can't be cleanly separated, what does the doctrine constrain?
  • 从「个人自由」出发：这些复杂制度的最终目的都是保护个人自由——三权分立是手段，不是目的。 Angle D (liberty): all this machinery ultimately serves individual liberty — separation of powers is a means, not an end.

2.1.10.2 Q2：政府体制是否「充分」平衡了权力？ Does the system adequately balance powers?

This asks for a position + argument. Key: "adequately" means "good enough," not "perfect." Positions:

  • 立场 1（推荐）基本充分但非完美：它防止了最坏情况（暴政），虽然个案仍有争议——「动态的、不完美的平衡」。 Position 1 (recommended): it prevents the worst case (tyranny) even if individual cases stay contested — a dynamic, imperfect balance.
  • 立场 2 不够充分：行政权膨胀：行政命令、对外军事、紧急状态、行政机构制定有约束力规章——「行政」越来越像「立法」。 Position 2: executive/administrative power has grown beyond the framers' design; agencies both make and enforce rules.
  • 立场 3 不够充分：司法被动有盲区：法院只能在「有案子告上来」且有人有起诉资格时才能介入，留下制衡空白。 Position 3: judicial review is passive — it only works when a case is brought and someone has standing.
  • 立场 4 动态问题：「平衡」随战争、危机与各分支相对强弱周期性变化，不是常数。 Position 4: the balance is cyclical — it shifts with war, crisis, and each branch's relative strength.
  • 立场 5 比较视角：相对议会制（行政立法合一，如英国），美国刚性分权提供更强结构制衡。 Position 5 (comparative): vs. a parliamentary system where executive and legislature are fused, the U.S. provides a stronger structural check.

推荐成稿思路：先答 Q1（最触动的是「野心对抗野心」——政府是被设计来应对人性弱点的），再答 Q2（充分但非完美：水门案证明可防暴政，行政机构例子却揭示边界模糊，现代行政权已远超制宪者设想——结论是「动态且不完美的平衡」）。

Suggested framing: answer Q1 first (what struck me was "ambition counteracting ambition" — government designed around human fallibility), then Q2 (yes but imperfectly: Watergate shows it blocks tyranny, while the agency example reveals a genuinely blurry boundary and modern executive power has grown beyond the founders' imagination — a dynamic, imperfect balance).

2.2立法分支 · Legislative Branch

▶2.2.1宪法第一条：国会结构 Article I — Congress

宪法第一条（Article I）设计立法分支。核心思想：两院制（bicameral）、权力分立与制衡、议员选举方式、立法程序，以及国会拥有的权力。

Article I designs the legislative branch. Its central ideas: bicameralism, the separation of powers (checks and balances), the election of Senators and Representatives, the lawmaking process, and the powers Congress holds.

2.2.1.1 第一款：两院制 Section 1 — bicameral

「所有立法权归于合众国国会」——由参议院与众议院两院组成（all legislative powers vested in a Congress of a Senate and House of Representatives）。

"All legislative powers herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a Senate and House of Representatives."

2.2.1.2 众议院（第二款） House (Section 2)

  • 议员每 2 年由各州人民选举产生。 Members chosen every second year by the people.
  • 资格：年满 25 岁、成为合众国公民 7 年、当选时须为该州居民。 At least 25 years old, 7 years a citizen, inhabitant of the state.
  • 众议员与直接税按各州人口分配（每 10 年人口普查一次）。 Apportioned by population, with a census every ten years.
  • 众议院独享弹劾权（sole power of impeachment）。 The House has the sole power of impeachment.

2.2.1.3 参议院（第三款） Senate (Section 3)

  • 每州 2 名参议员，任期 6 年，每人 1 票。 Two Senators per state, six-year terms, one vote each.
  • 资格：年满 30 岁、公民 9 年、该州居民。 At least 30, nine years a citizen, inhabitant of the state.
  • 副总统为参议院议长，但除非票数相等不得投票。 The Vice President presides but votes only to break a tie.
  • 参议院独享审理弹劾案之权；定罪须出席议员 2/3 同意；弹劾判决仅限于免职。 The Senate has sole power to try impeachments; conviction requires two-thirds; judgment extends only to removal from office.

This is the module's central learning objective. The standard (idealized) process has seven steps:

Article I, Section 7 (the Presentment Clause): a bill must pass both houses and be presented to the President; a veto can be overridden by a two-thirds vote in each house.

▶2.2.4国会的权力 Powers of Congress
  • 宣战（declare war）
  • 制定国家预算（enact the national budget / power of the purse）
  • 确认行政官员（参议院确认：confirm executive officers）

Key limit: the Constitution is a "document of grant" — Congress holds only enumerated powers and cannot exercise unenumerated ones; the Necessary and Proper Clause still must serve an enumerated end.

2.3行政分支 · Executive Branch

▶2.3.1总统权力的性质与范围 Nature & scope of presidential power

宪法第二条（Article II）把行政权交给总统——执行法律是行政权的核心。总统 + 副总统 + 内阁负责执行法律，行政分支雇员超 400 万。

Article II vests the executive power — and the execution of the laws — in the President. The President, Vice President, and Cabinet enforce the law; the branch employs over four million people.

2.3.1.1 总统职权 Presidential powers

  • 统帅军队（Commander-in-Chief）
  • 任命官员、赦免、缔结条约（须参议院批准）
  • 「使法律得以忠实执行」（take care that the laws be faithfully executed）
▶2.3.2行政命令（Executive Order） Executive orders

行政命令是总统签发的、管理联邦政府运作的书面指令。自华盛顿以来已累计超过 13,731 份（截至教材编写时）。

An executive order is a signed, written, published directive from the President managing operations of the federal government — over 13,731 have been issued since Washington.

2.3.2.1 是什么 / 不是什么 What it is and isn't

  • 有法律效力（force of law），与联邦机构规章类似，编入《联邦规章法典》第 3 编（Title 3）。 Executive orders have the force of law, like agency regulations, codified under Title 3 of the CFR.
  • 不是立法：无需国会批准，国会也不能简单推翻（可取消拨款使其难执行）。 They are not legislation: no congressional approval, and Congress cannot simply overturn them (though it may remove funding).
  • 只有现任总统可通过发布另一份行政命令来推翻既有命令。 Only a sitting President can overturn an existing order by issuing another.
  • 发布于《联邦公报》（Federal Register），并归档于国家档案局。 Published in the Federal Register and catalogued by the National Archives.

2.3.2.2 文档结构 Anatomy of an order

五部分：① 标题/编号与日期；② 标题；③ 引言（「依据宪法与法律授予我作为总统的权力……」）；④ 正文（分节）；⑤ 签名。命令按编号连续排列（1907 年起由国务院编号）。

Five components: heading (number + date), title, introduction ("by the authority vested in me as President…"), body (in numbered sections), and signature. Orders are numbered consecutively (State Dept. began numbering in 1907).

2.3.2.3 核心限制 The core limit

行政命令不得与制定法冲突；若处于灰色地带，最终由法院决定总统是否越权。近期案例：拜登学生贷款减免（被推翻）、新冠驱逐暂停令（被一致推翻）、DACA（4–4 平局）、特朗普穆斯林禁令（获维持）。

Orders cannot conflict with statutes; where ambiguous, courts decide whether the President exceeded his authority. Recent examples: Biden's student-loan forgiveness (struck down), the COVID eviction moratorium (struck down unanimously), DACA (4–4 tie), and the "Muslim ban" (upheld).

▶2.3.3总统豁免（Trump v. United States, 2024） Presidential immunity
Trump v. United States（2024）

特朗普因 2020 年大选后的行为被联邦起诉。最高法院（Roberts 执笔，6–3）确立三层豁免框架：

Trump was federally indicted for post-2020-election conduct. The Court (Roberts, 6–3) announced a three-tier immunity framework:

层级范围豁免
① 核心宪法权力「结论性与排他性」权力（如司法部、赦免）绝对豁免
② 其他官方行为其他职务行为推定豁免（政府可反驳）
③ 非官方行为私人/非职务行为无豁免

关键规则：判断行为是否「官方」时，法院不得探究总统动机；获得豁免的行为不得作为庭审证据。法院同时驳回特朗普「须先经弹劾定罪」的主张，也驳回政府「前总统完全无豁免」的主张。

Key rules: courts may not inquire into the President's motives when deciding if conduct is official, and immunized conduct may not be admitted as evidence. The Court rejected both Trump's impeachment-first argument and the Government's no-immunity argument.

▶2.3.4弹劾（Impeachment） Impeachment

弹劾是「我们人民」经民选代表制衡政府官员的宪法机制——官员可因叛国、贿赂或其他重罪轻罪（treason, bribery, or other high crimes and misdemeanors）被指控并受审。

Impeachment is the constitutional mechanism by which "we the people," through our elected representatives, check government officials — for treason, bribery, or other high crimes and misdemeanors.

  • 众议院起草并通过弹劾条款（articles of impeachment）。 The House drafts and votes on articles of impeachment.
  • 参议院进行审判；被告可请律师、传唤证人。 The Senate conducts the trial; the accused may be represented and call witnesses.
  • 定罪须出席参议员 2/3 同意。 Conviction requires two-thirds of Senators present.
  • 唯一惩罚是免职——弹劾既非刑事也非民事程序，是否坐牢/罚款只能由法院另判。 The sole penalty is removal from office — impeachment is neither criminal nor civil; prison or fines are for courts alone.

实例：1990 年代众议院曾对克林顿总统起草弹劾条款；此后弹劾主要针对联邦法官。

Example: the House drafted articles against President Clinton in the 1990s; since then impeachment has targeted mostly federal judges.

▶2.3.5对总统权力的制衡 Checks on presidential power
  • 立法制衡：参议院拒绝批准任命/条约、国会拒绝拨款、弹劾。 Legislative: Senate rejects nominations/treaties, Congress defunds, impeachment.
  • 司法制衡：法院审查行政命令是否合宪、是否与制定法一致（如 Nixon 录音带案）。 Judicial: courts review whether executive orders are consistent with the Constitution and statutes (e.g., the Nixon tapes).
  • 制衡理念：总统不能凌驾于法律之上——「使法律得以忠实执行」义务与司法审查共同约束行政权。 No president is above the law — the take-care duty and judicial review constrain the executive.

2.4司法分支 · Judicial Branch

▶2.4.1联邦法院结构 Federal court structure

宪法第三条（Article III）设立司法分支——最高法院及国会可设立的下级法院。三级结构：

Article III creates the judicial branch — the Supreme Court and such inferior courts as Congress establishes. Three tiers:

法院数量职能
美国最高法院（Supreme Court）1最高司法机关，解释宪法与法律
巡回上诉法院（Courts of Appeals）13审查下级法院/行政机构是否正确适用法律（无陪审团）
地区法院（District Courts）94初审法院：查明事实 + 适用法律
破产法院（Bankruptcy Courts）90处理无力偿债，给个人/企业「重新开始」

2.4.1.1 第一条法院（Article I courts） Article I courts

国会依第一条设立的「立法法院」，不属于第三分支：退伍军人上诉法院、武装部队上诉法院、税务法院、破产法院等。注意：国会可以设立此类法院（Quiz 高频考点）。

Congress creates Article I (legislative) courts outside the Third Branch: veterans claims, armed forces, tax court, bankruptcy courts. Note: Congress may create them — a common quiz point.

▶2.4.2最高法院（SCOTUS） The Supreme Court
  • 9 名大法官：1 名首席大法官（Chief Justice）+ 8 名联席大法官（Associate Justices）。 Nine justices: one Chief Justice and eight Associate Justices.
  • 由总统提名、经参议院「建议与同意」（advice and consent）确认。 Nominated by the President and confirmed with the Senate's advice and consent.
  • 终身任职（"good behavior"），除非被弹劾并定罪。 Life tenure during "good behavior," removable only by impeachment and conviction.
  • 唯一由宪法直接设立的法院（第三条）；其他联邦法院由国会设立。 The only court the Constitution itself creates; all other federal courts are created by Congress.
  • 每年开庭期始于10 月第一个星期一，多数判决在次年 6 月中发布。 Term begins the first Monday in October; most decisions come in mid-June.
▶2.4.3法官如何判案（How do judges judge?） Judicial behavior

核心问题：法官为什么会这样判？是否在「政治地」裁判？法学家、政治学家争论数十年。

The core question: why do judges decide as they do — do they behave politically?

2.4.3.1 可能动机 Possible motivations

  • 意识形态（自由/保守方向）
  • 对任命者（总统/州长）的忠诚
  • 经济学式的效率考量
  • 随机性

2.4.3.2 实证发现 Empirical findings

法官意识形态（以任命总统的党籍衡量）与高度意识形态化案件的结果有较强相关性——民主党总统任命的法官更倾向支持堕胎权，共和党任命的更倾向支持持枪权。但相关性并不完美：

A judge's ideology (measured by the appointing president's party) correlates fairly strongly with outcomes in ideologically charged cases — Democratic appointees favor abortion rights, Republican appointees gun rights. But the correlation is imperfect:

  • 艾森豪威尔（共和党）任命的 Earl Warren、William Brennan 极为自由派，他称之为「任内最大的错误」。 Eisenhower's Warren and Brennan proved very liberal — his "biggest mistakes."
  • 老布什任命的 David Souter、里根任命的 Anthony Kennedy（同性婚姻决定性一票）都令任命者「失望」。 H.W. Bush's Souter and Reagan's Kennedy (the same-sex marriage swing vote) disappointed their appointees.

2.4.3.3 策略行为 Strategic behavior

多数法院是多人机构（最高法院 9 人、上诉法院 3 人合议庭）——结果取决于能否凑成多数（最高法院需 5 票）。法官可能策略性交换选票（vote trading）。

Courts are multimember bodies (9 on the Court, 3-judge panels on appeal) — outcomes depend on assembling a majority (five votes). Judges may act tactically, even trading votes.

▶2.4.4法官应如何判案（How should judges judge?） Normative question

我们是否接受法官「政治地」判案，取决于我们的判案哲学（philosophy of judging）与政府哲学。

Whether we accept political judging depends on our philosophy of judging and of government.

2.4.4.1 终身制的后果 Life tenure's consequence

联邦法官基本不受政治/选举控制（终身任职、极难罢免），因此即便「按政治标准判案」也几乎不可能被弹劾免职——这留下一个「颇具争议的司法裁量空间」。

Federal judges are largely impervious to political and electoral control (life tenure), so even "political" judging almost never leads to impeachment — leaving a controversial space of judicial discretion.

2.4.4.2 制衡视角 The checks-and-balances view

总统交替上台决定法官人选，因此「如何任命法官」是选举总统的重要考量；对「政治判案」的异议也许更应指向任命法官的总统与确认法官的参议院，而非法官本人。

Presidents alternate in power, so how a president will appoint judges is a key electoral consideration; objections to political judging may be better aimed at the appointing president and confirming Senate than at the judge.

2.4.4.3 法官也是治理者 Judges as co-governors

法官与国会、总统一样宣誓忠于法律；但法律有模糊与空白，法官须行使判断。历史上的法官常凭「我们人民的信念」做出与时代精神一致的判决。

Judges take the same oath of legal fidelity as Congress and the President; but law has ambiguity and gaps, and judges must exercise judgment — historically often consistent with "we the people's" convictions.

2.5司法审查 · Judicial Review

▶2.5.1谁来守护守护者（Who guards the guardians?） Who guards the guardians?

宪法对联邦与州的权力设限，但如果政府违宪行事，谁来执行这些限制？——「谁来守护守护者？」答案在 1803 年的 Marbury v. Madison。

The Constitution sets limits on government, but if the government acts unconstitutionally, who enforces those limits? The answer came in Marbury v. Madison (1803).

马歇尔首席大法官那句名言：「阐明法律是什么，是司法部门的职责与专属领域。」（It is emphatically the province and duty of the judicial department to say what the law is.）由此确立司法审查：法院有权解释宪法、并宣告违宪的政府行为无效。

Chief Justice Marshall: "It is emphatically the province and duty of the judicial department to say what the law is." This established judicial review — the power of courts to interpret the Constitution and invalidate unconstitutional governmental action.

▶2.5.2Marbury v. Madison 详解（1803） Marbury v. Madison in detail
案情 Facts

1800 年大选亚当斯（联邦党）败给杰斐逊（民主共和党）。卸任前国会通过《1801 年司法法》，亚当斯赶在最后时刻任命一批「午夜法官」（midnight judges）。马伯里的委任状未及送达，新任国务卿麦迪逊拒不发，马伯里依《1789 年司法法》第 13 条直接诉至最高法院，请求执行令（writ of mandamus）。

After Adams (Federalist) lost the 1800 election to Jefferson (Democratic-Republican), Congress passed the Judiciary Act of 1801 and Adams made last-minute "midnight" appointments. Marbury's commission was never delivered; new Secretary of State Madison refused; Marbury sued under §13 of the Judiciary Act of 1789 for a writ of mandamus.

2.5.2.1 判决 Holding

马歇尔三步：① 马伯里有权得到委任；② 法律应给救济；③ 但《司法法》第 13 条授予最高法院的初审管辖权与宪法第三条第二款相抵触，违宪无效——故法院无权下令。结果：马伯里「谁都没赢」。

Marshall's three steps: (1) Marbury had a right to the commission; (2) the law must afford a remedy; (3) but §13's grant of original jurisdiction conflicted with Article III, §2 and was void — so the Court lacked power to order delivery. Technically, nobody won.

2.5.2.2 深意 Significance

马歇尔借「不给自己权力」的判决，确立更大的权力——司法审查：宪法是最高法（supreme law of the land），与之抵触的法律无效。既避免与杰斐逊正面冲突，又为最高法院（联邦党立场）争得权力。

By declining to exercise power, Marshall established a greater one — judicial review: the Constitution is supreme, and a law repugnant to it is void. It avoided a clash with Jefferson while empowering the Court.

▶2.5.3司法审查的演进 Evolution of judicial review

司法审查在美国体系里很「激进」——英国至今把「是否违宪」的专属判断权放在立法/行政分支。而美国由法院保留「推翻任何违宪政府行为的最终权力」，它「或许是对政府权力最重要的制衡」。

Judicial review was radical — the U.K. still leaves the exclusive prerogative to determine constitutionality with the legislative/executive branches. In America, courts keep the ultimate power to invalidate unconstitutional action — "perhaps the single most important check on governmental power."

2.5.3.1 历史脉络 Historical arc

  • 建国后长达一个多世纪，法院更常推翻的是州立法，很少推翻国会立法。 For over a century courts struck down state laws far more often than federal ones.
  • Dred Scott（1857）：罕见的例外——法院推翻《密苏里妥协案》（涉奴隶制），反而加速了内战。 Dred Scott (1857) struck down the Missouri Compromise and hastened the Civil War.
  • 直到20 世纪，法院才开始较频繁地以违宪为由推翻国会立法。 Not until the 20th century did courts frequently strike down acts of Congress.

2.5.3.2 里程碑 Landmarks

  • Brown v. Board of Education（1954）：推翻「隔离但平等」的公立教育种族隔离。 Rejected "separate but equal" in public education.
  • Cooper v. Aaron（1958）：明确州政府官员（立法、行政、司法）必须遵守联邦法院对宪法的解释，终结了「各州自行解释宪法」的主张。 Made clear that state officials must follow federal courts' constitutional interpretations, ending state "nullification."
▶2.5.4反多数难题 The counter-majoritarian difficulty

司法审查「反多数」（counter-majoritarian）：它赋予非民选的联邦法官推翻民选代表决定的权力，可能干预民主选择。这是 Bickel 提出的经典难题（课上由同学 Keshav 提出）。

Judicial review is "counter-majoritarian" — it gives unelected judges power to override democratic choice (Bickel's classic difficulty, raised in class by Keshav).

2.5.4.1 两种回应 Two responses

  • 主张司法审查正当：美国不是纯粹民主，而是民主共和（democratic republic）——必须对「民主的过度」设司法强制之限（「民主的防波堤」）。 Defense: America is a democratic republic, not pure democracy — judicial limits on democratic excess are appropriate ("a bulwark against democracy").
  • 主张司法审查不反多数：法院总体上会与民主原则一致，法官虽非民选，却能「充分理解我们人民所要」，是代议制政府的又一道制度保障。 Alternative: courts on balance act consistently with democratic principles; unelected judges still carry out the people's will — another institutional guarantee of representative government.

2.6同步课精讲（2026年9月1日）· Sync Session (Sep 1, 2026)

▶2.6.1法律渊源全景 The full landscape of sources of law

教授系统梳理了美国法律渊源的完整层级，特别强调「宪法」是复数——美国有 51 部宪法（联邦 + 各州）。

The professor mapped the full hierarchy of American sources of law, stressing that "constitutions" is plural — the U.S. has 51 constitutions (federal plus the states).

2.6.1.1 宪法（51 部） Constitutions

联邦宪法 1787 年制定（算上权利法案为 1789），世界最古老，仅 7,762 词，240 多年只修 27 次（其中第 18/21 修正案相抵，净修约 14 次）。各州还有自己的宪法，属该州根本法、但从属于联邦宪法。

The U.S. Constitution (1787/1789) is the world's oldest, just 7,762 words, amended only 27 times in 240 years. Each state also has its own constitution — fundamental law within that state, but subordinate to the U.S. Constitution.

2.6.1.2 制定法（statutes） Statutes

只有立法机关能制定：联邦是国会，州是州议会。总统、州长不能制定法律。

Only legislatures enact statutes — Congress federally, state legislatures in the states. Presidents and governors cannot make statutes.

2.6.1.3 条约 / 行政命令 / 规章 Treaties, executive orders, regulations

  • 条约：权威依赖国会批准并转化为制定法。 Treaties rely on Congress ratifying and turning them into statute.
  • 行政命令：总统单方发布，须与其他法律渊源一致。 Executive orders are unilateral, but must be consistent with other sources of law.
  • 规章/规则：行政机构制定，有法律效力但不是制定法（非国会制定）。 Regulations come from agencies, have the force of law, but are not statutes.
▶2.6.2州宪法 State constitutions

州宪法比联邦宪法更长、更易变，还充满「奇怪」条款。

State constitutions are longer and far more changeable than the U.S. Constitution, and often stuffed with strange provisions.

2.6.2.1 更易变 More malleable

伊利诺伊有过 4 部宪法、路易斯安那 11 部；最新的伊利诺伊宪法才 1970 年制定（州本身却建于 19 世纪）。可经立法程序或「制宪会议」改换整部宪法。

Illinois has had four constitutions, Louisiana eleven; the current Illinois Constitution dates only from 1970. They change through the legislature or constitutional conventions.

2.6.2.2 更长、更「奇怪」 Longer & stranger

最短的州宪法（佛蒙特）也是联邦宪法的 2 倍多；阿拉巴马高达 34 万词。且塞满琐碎条款：德州禁无人取款机、纽约规定阿迪朗达克滑雪坡长度、加州「钓鱼权」、多州禁在宾果游戏上花钱。

Vermont (the shortest) is still over twice the U.S. Constitution; Alabama runs 340,000 words. States embed odd details — Texas bans unmanned teller machines, California has a right to fish.

2.6.2.3 加入联邦须先有宪法 Admission requires a constitution

各州加入联邦须先备好宪法（国会决定是否接纳）。犹他州因国会在其宪法中强制写入禁止一夫多妻而拖延多年才加入。

A constitution is essentially a precondition for statehood. Utah was delayed for years because Congress insisted its constitution prohibit polygamy.

▶2.6.3行政命令与制衡 Executive orders & checks

行政命令是总统施政的重要工具，但受法律与法院制衡。

Executive orders are a major tool of presidential governance, but they are checked by law and the courts.

2.6.3.1 历史与激增 History & the spike

自小布什起行政命令激增；奥巴马在国会僵局下「只要有笔」（as long as I have a pen）大量使用；特朗普、拜登沿用。罗斯福也曾发布极多。

The spike began under George W. Bush. After Congress gridlocked, Obama governed by "the pen" ("as long as I have a pen"), and Trump and Biden followed.

2.6.3.2 核心限制 The core limit

行政命令不得与制定法冲突；若未被制定法明确授权又处于灰色地带，最终往往由法院决定是否越权。

Executive orders cannot conflict with statutes; where they fall in a gray zone, the courts ultimately decide whether the President exceeded his authority.

2.6.3.3 法院制衡案例 Judicial checks — cases

  • 拜登学生贷款减免：被最高法院推翻（超出总统权限）。 Biden's student-loan forgiveness was struck down as beyond presidential authority.
  • 新冠驱逐暂停令：被最高法院一致推翻。 The COVID eviction moratorium was struck down unanimously.
  • 奥巴马 DACA：最高法院打成 4–4（一法官回避）。 Obama's DACA ended in a 4–4 tie after a recusal.
  • 特朗普穆斯林禁令：Trump v. Hawaii 中获维持。 Trump's "Muslim ban" was upheld in Trump v. Hawaii.

制衡来源：国会设「护栏」＋法院审查其是否合宪、是否与制定法一致。

Checks come from Congress imposing guardrails, and from courts reviewing whether orders are consistent with the Constitution and statutes.

▶2.6.4宪法作为社会契约 The Constitution as social contract

宪法可被理解为一种「社会契约」——与「我们人民」订立（源自同学 Robert 的提问与教授回应）。

The Constitution can be understood as a social contract with "We the People" (prompted by a student's question and the professor's response).

2.6.4.1 思想源头 Intellectual roots

洛克、卢梭、孟德斯鸠、麦迪逊都谈及「社会契约」；政府核心责任是落实人民意志（人民主权，popular sovereignty）。

Locke, Rousseau, Montesquieu, and Madison all spoke of the social contract; the government's core duty is to carry out the people's will (popular sovereignty).

2.6.4.2 与谁订立？ With whom?

序言写「我们人民」（We the People），但教授指出这种关系「主要通过州」运行——州代表人民行使权威、起草宪法。契约涉及的是公民，却经由「联邦政府 ↔ 州政府」这层关系落地。

The Preamble says "We the People," but the professor noted the relationship runs largely through the states, which exercise authority on behalf of the people and draft constitutions.

2.6.4.3 只是隐喻 Metaphor, not contract

这是隐喻而非真实契约——不具备合同的可执行性与机制，主要在理论层面有启发。

It is a metaphor, not an actual contract — lacking the enforceability and mechanics of a real contract, useful mainly at the level of theory.

▶2.6.5制度为何「刻意不民主」 Deliberately undemocratic institutions

许多制度是刻意反民主的，作为对「民主过度」的制度化约束。

Many institutions are deliberately undemocratic, acting as institutional checks on democratic excess.

2.6.5.1 参议院 The Senate

每州 2 票、不论人口（蒙大拿 = 加州），是「典型的不民主机构」（classically undemocratic institution）——按「州」而非按「人口」代表，保护小州利益（「大妥协」）。

Two votes per state regardless of population makes the Senate a "classically undemocratic institution" — representing states as states (the Great Compromise).

2.6.5.2 联邦司法 The federal judiciary

联邦法院被设计成「民主的一道防波堤」（a bulwark against democracy）——非民选、终身制，用以约束民主。

Federal courts were intended as "a bulwark against democracy" — unelected and life-tenured, meant to constrain democracy.

2.6.5.3 反多数难题 Counter-majoritarian difficulty

同学 Keshav 提出「反多数难题」（源自阅读 Bickel）：非民选法院制衡民选政府，是否偏离民主？教授答「guilty as charged」——这正是「民主宪政/共和政府」的用意：我们不是纯粹民主。

Keshav raised the counter-majoritarian difficulty (from Bickel): is an unelected court checking elected branches a step away from democracy? The professor answered "guilty as charged" — that is the point of democratic constitutionalism.

▶2.6.6普通法精讲 Common law deep dive

教授用侵权法例子详解「法官造法」与先例机制。

The professor used a torts example to explain judge-made law and the machinery of precedent.

2.6.6.1 法官造法 Judge-made law

法律规则来自司法意见（非制定法/规章）。例：侵权法（责任法）里的共同过失（contributory negligence）——原告只要有丝毫过错就完全不能获赔。

The legal rule comes from a judicial opinion, not a statute. E.g., contributory negligence: any fault by the plaintiff bars recovery entirely.

2.6.6.2 规则会演化 Rules evolve

后来多数州改用比较过失（comparative negligence）——按过错比例分配（如闯红灯者 70% / 酒驾者 30%）。高院可改规则，但需「发现更好的原则」。

Most states later adopted comparative negligence — apportioning blame (e.g., 70/30). A high court may revise the rule, "discovering" a better principle.

2.6.6.3 先例 / 区分 / 溯及力 Precedent, distinguishing, retroactivity

  • 先例（stare decisis）：高院确立的规则拘束下级法院。 A high court's rule binds lower courts.
  • 区分案件：主张规则只适用某类事实（如仅限车祸，不含产品责任）。 Distinguishing: the rule applies only to certain facts.
  • 溯及力：普通法是「发现」法律故溯及既往；立法是「创造」新法故仅及将来。 Common law "discovers" law, so it applies retroactively; legislation "makes" new law, so only prospectively.
▶2.6.7异议与协同意见 Dissenting & concurring opinions

只有多数意见有拘束力；异议与协同意见是美国独特传统。

Only the majority opinion binds; dissents and concurrences are a uniquely American practice.

2.6.7.1 多数意见 Majority opinion

最高法院需 5 票才构成有拘束力的多数；不足 5 票的「复数意见」（plurality）只有结果、无拘束规则。

The Supreme Court needs five votes for a binding majority; a plurality (less than five) settles the outcome but not the rule.

2.6.7.2 协同意见 Concurrence

同意结果、但想另行说明理由（如 Gorsuch 常写超长协同意见）。

Joining the result but writing separately with a different rationale (e.g., Gorsuch's lengthy concurrences).

2.6.7.3 异议意见 Dissent

「来日再战」（live to fight another day）——向历史、向后人发声，可能在未来成为多数。历史上异议常变多数。美国特色：别国异议极罕见，美国法官却「要讲个明白」。

Dissents "live to fight another day" — speaking to history and the future, sometimes becoming the majority later. This is quintessentially American; elsewhere dissents are rare.

▶2.6.8开放条款与解释难题 Open-ended provisions & the difficulty of interpretation

这是同步课的重点：开放条款文字给不出答案，被教授称为「极其开放」「令法院一筹莫展」。

The heart of the session: open-ended provisions give courts almost nothing to grab onto — "tremendously open-ended" and "confounding," as the professor put it.

2.6.8.1 正当程序 Due process

分程序性（什么程序才够）与实体性。先问是否「剥夺生命/自由/财产」（福利金、终身教职都算）；再问「给什么程序」——是否需要听证、能否交叉质证。

Split into procedural (what process is due) and substantive. First, is there a deprivation of life/liberty/property (welfare benefits, tenured jobs)? Then, what process is owed — a hearing? cross-examination?

2.6.8.2 残酷异常刑罚 Cruel and unusual punishment

毒气室、枪决、电椅→今注射；对未成年人判终身不得假释被认定违宪；Grants Pass 案（反露营法针对无家可归者）最高法院推翻第九巡回法院、认为不构成残酷异常刑罚。

Gas chambers, firing squads, the electric chair gave way to lethal injection; juvenile life-without-parole was held unconstitutional; in Grants Pass the Court reversed the Ninth Circuit on an anti-camping ordinance.

2.6.8.3 不合理搜查 Unreasonable searches

「令状要求」本身是法官造出来的（宪法未明文）；Carpenter 案：基站定位数据算不算「搜查」；核心标准是「合理隐私期待」（reasonable expectation of privacy）。

The warrant requirement is itself judge-made; Carpenter asked whether cell-tower location data is a "search"; the touchstone is a "reasonable expectation of privacy."

2.6.8.4 宗教条款 Religion clauses

第一修正案两条款方向相反：自由行使 vs 政教分离。Employment v. Smith（斯卡利亚）确立「中立且普遍适用」原则——只要不针对特定宗教即可（如圆顶小帽、佩奥特碱、一夫多妻）。

The First Amendment's two clauses pull in opposite directions — free exercise vs. establishment. Employment Division v. Smith (Scalia) set the "neutral and generally applicable" test (yarmulkes, peyote, polygamy).

▶2.6.9时代变迁与先例 Changing times & precedent (Raich)

先例有拘束力，但「时代变了」，判决也可能随之改变。

Precedent binds, but "times change," and so may decisions.

2.6.9.1 Raich 案回顾 Revisiting Raich

Gonzales v. Raich（2005）：联邦管制自种自用医用大麻合宪。教授用它说明「先例 vs 时代变迁」的张力。

Gonzales v. Raich (2005) upheld federal regulation of homegrown medical marijuana — the professor used it to illustrate precedent versus changing times.

2.6.9.2 史蒂文斯访谈 Stevens's oral history

教授分享：采访已故大法官史蒂文斯（西北校友）时，他几乎承认「我未必还会那样判」——今天大麻去管制化的背景（或从 Schedule I 降到 Schedule III）让当年判决显得「是时代的产物」。

The professor recalled that in an oral history with Justice Stevens (a Northwestern graduate), Stevens all but said "I'm not sure I'd make the same decision" — as federal marijuana policy moves to de-schedule, Raich looks like a product of its times.
